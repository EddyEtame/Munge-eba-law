# Hero media specification

The production hero should use approved footage of the actual office. The former generated exterior, threshold and interior stills were rejected and have been removed from the active site.

## Required source material

- One stable exterior shot showing the real building and its Bepanda context.
- One continuous approach to the correct entrance and a genuine door opening.
- One interior arrival shot showing the real client-facing office space.
- Written confirmation that the office, people and visible materials may appear on the website.

## Final edit

- Duration: 5–7 seconds.
- Sequence: exterior hold (at least 0.7 seconds), approach, door opening, interior settle.
- The first visible poster frame must be identical to the first video frame.
- No artificial signage and no model-generated version of the logo. If the logo is composited, use the exact supplied artwork.
- No audio track.
- Avoid faces, files, screens, case names and other confidential material.

## Delivery files

- `office-entrance-desktop.webm` — 1920×1080, VP9 or AV1, target under 6 MB.
- `office-entrance-desktop.mp4` — 1920×1080, H.264 fallback, target under 8 MB.
- `office-entrance-mobile.webm` — 1080×1920, portrait edit, target under 5 MB.
- `office-entrance-mobile.mp4` — 1080×1920, H.264 fallback, target under 7 MB.
- `office-entrance-desktop-poster.webp` and `office-entrance-mobile-poster.webp` — exact first frames.

Until approved footage is supplied, the live hero uses the real logo with a short, bounded WebGL light pass. It does not represent generated stills as video.
